Hardware Sensors Used

Physical sensors integrated with the ESP32 microcontroller and their operational functions.

pH Sensor Analog Probe

Measures the hydrogen-ion concentration (acidity or alkalinity) of wastewater to ensure effluent remains within the safe 6.5 – 8.5 pH regulatory discharge range.

Field: field1 Pinout: GPIO 34 (ADC1_CH6)
TDS Sensor Analog Probe

Measures dissolved minerals, inorganic salts, and electrical conductivity in parts per million (ppm) to evaluate water mineralization and purity.

Field: field2 Pinout: GPIO 35 (ADC1_CH7)
Turbidity Sensor Optoelectronic

Uses optical light transmittance and scattering to quantify suspended solid particles and cloudiness in the effluent (measured in NTU).

Field: field3 Pinout: GPIO 32 (ADC1_CH4)
Temperature Sensor Digital 1-Wire

Monitors wastewater temperature (using a waterproof DS18B20 probe) to prevent thermal pollution and protect aquatic ecosystems from hot industrial discharge.

Field: field4 Pinout: GPIO 4 (1-Wire Bus)
